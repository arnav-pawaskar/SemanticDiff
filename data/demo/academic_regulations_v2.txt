UNIVERSITY ACADEMIC REGULATIONS 2026

Section 1 Scope
1.1 These regulations apply to all undergraduate students enrolled in the University.
1.2 The Academic Council may amend these regulations at the start of each semester.
1.3 Students must carry their identity cards on campus.

Section 2 Definitions
2.1 "Eligible Student" means a student maintaining at least 85% attendance in every course.
2.2 "Academic Year" means the period from 1 July to 30 June of the following year.
2.3 "Examination Committee" means the committee constituted by the Registrar.

Section 3 Attendance
3.1 All students should maintain 70% attendance.
3.2 Students with medical exemptions may not appear for examinations.
3.3 The student portal shall display attendance records once every fortnight.
3.4 Students with pending disciplinary cases require special approval to appear for examinations.

Section 4 Examinations
4.1 Only Eligible Students may register for end-semester examinations.
4.2 Applicants are required to submit their re-evaluation applications within one week of the declaration of results.
4.3 The re-evaluation fee is Rs. 750 per course.
4.4 Final-year students must submit applications for grade improvement within 15 days with faculty approval.

Section 5 Hostel
5.1 Hostel accommodation is available to all Eligible Students.
5.2 Residents must vacate their rooms within 15 working days of the end of the Academic Year.
5.3 Visitors are prohibited from entering the hostel on weekends.

Section 6 Library
6.1 Students may access the laboratory and library facilities on all working days.

Section 7 Conduct
7.1 Ragging in any form is strictly prohibited.

Section 8 Scholarships
8.1 Scholarships are available to all Eligible Students.
8.2 Scholarship recipients must maintain a minimum CGPA of 8.0.
8.3 The scholarship amount is Rs. 25,000 per semester.
8.4 Students receive travel reimbursement for presenting papers at conferences if approved by their faculty advisor.

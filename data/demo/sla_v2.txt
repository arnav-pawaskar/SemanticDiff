SERVICE LEVEL AGREEMENT

1. Availability
1.1 The service shall be available 99.5% of the time in each calendar month.
1.2 Scheduled maintenance must not exceed 240 minutes per month.

2. Performance
2.1 The server shall return a response within half a second.
2.2 The provider shall notify the customer of any outage within 60 minutes.
2.3 Support tickets should be acknowledged within 48 hours.

3. Service Credits
3.1 The customer is entitled to a service credit of 10% of the monthly fee if availability falls below 99.5%.
3.2 Credit requests must be submitted within 30 days of the incident, subject to verification by the provider.

4. Termination
4.1 Either party may terminate this agreement with 30 days written notice.
4.2 The customer shall pay all outstanding fees before termination.

UNIVERSITY ACADEMIC REGULATIONS 2025

Section 1 Scope
1.1 These regulations apply to all undergraduate students enrolled in the University.
1.2 The Academic Council may amend these regulations at the start of each semester.

Section 2 Definitions
2.1 "Eligible Student" means a student maintaining at least 75% attendance in every course.
2.2 "Academic Year" means the period from 1 July to 30 June of the following year.
2.3 "Examination Committee" means the committee constituted by the Dean of Academics.

Section 3 Attendance
3.1 All students must maintain 75% attendance.
3.2 Students with medical exemptions may appear for examinations.
3.3 Attendance records shall be published on the student portal every two weeks.

Section 4 Examinations
4.1 Only Eligible Students may register for end-semester examinations.
4.2 Applications for re-evaluation shall be submitted within seven days of the declaration of results.
4.3 The re-evaluation fee is Rs. 500 per course.
4.4 All students may submit applications for grade improvement within 30 days.

Section 5 Hostel
5.1 Hostel accommodation is available to all Eligible Students.
5.2 Residents must vacate their rooms within 15 days of the end of the Academic Year.
5.3 Visitors are allowed to enter the hostel on weekends.

Section 6 Library
6.1 Students may access the laboratory and library facilities on all working days.
6.2 Borrowed books must be returned within 14 days.

Section 7 Conduct
7.1 Students must carry their identity cards on campus.
7.2 Ragging in any form is strictly prohibited.

Section 8 Scholarships
8.1 Scholarships are available to all Eligible Students.
8.2 Scholarship recipients must maintain a minimum CGPA of 7.5.
8.3 The scholarship amount is Rs. 25,000 per semester.
8.4 Students receive travel reimbursement for presenting papers at conferences.

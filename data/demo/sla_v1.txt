SERVICE LEVEL AGREEMENT

1. Availability
1.1 The service must be available 99.9% of the time in each calendar month.
1.2 Scheduled maintenance shall not exceed 4 hours per month.

2. Performance
2.1 The server must respond within 500 milliseconds.
2.2 The provider shall notify the customer of any outage within one hour.
2.3 Support tickets must be acknowledged within 24 hours.

3. Service Credits
3.1 The customer is entitled to a service credit of 10% of the monthly fee if availability falls below 99.9%.
3.2 Credit requests must be submitted within 30 days of the incident.

4. Termination
4.1 Either party may terminate this agreement with 60 days written notice.
4.2 The customer shall pay all outstanding fees before termination.
